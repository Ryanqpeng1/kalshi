## How to Trade

1. **Get Balance**: Check your account with `python get_balance.py`
2. **Browse Markets**: See all available markets with `python browse_markets.py`
3. **Place Order**: Trade with `python place_order.py [TICKER] [SIDE] [ACTION] [COUNT] [PRICE]`

### Example Orders:
```bash
# Buy 5 contracts betting YES on the NYC temp market at 50 cents
python place_order.py KXTEMPNYCH-26MAY2604-T70.99 yes buy 5 50

# Sell 3 contracts betting NO on a tennis match at 45 cents
python place_order.py KXITFMATCH-26MAY26CLAJOH-JOH no sell 3 45
```
